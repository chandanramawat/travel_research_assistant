# Tripsaarthi Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Flight Bookings

- Cancellations made **more than 7 days** before departure: full refund minus a processing fee of ₹500.
- Cancellations made **between 3 and 7 days** before departure: 50% refund of the ticket amount.
- Cancellations made **less than 72 hours** before departure: no refund, only government taxes are refunded.
- Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days to the original payment method.

## Hotel Bookings

- Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before check-in for most properties.
- Non-refundable rate bookings cannot be cancelled or refunded under any circumstances.
- If a hotel cancels a confirmed booking due to unavailability, Tripsaarthi provides a full refund plus a 10% travel credit as compensation.

## Package Tours

- Cancellations made 15+ days before the tour start date: 90% refund.
- Cancellations made 7-14 days before: 50% refund.
- Cancellations made less than 7 days before: no refund.
- Visa fees and any third-party vendor charges already paid are non-refundable at any stage.

## How to Request a Refund

1. Go to "My Bookings" in the Tripsaarthi app.
2. Select the booking and click "Cancel Booking."
3. Refund status can be tracked under "Refund History."
4. For payment failures where money was deducted but booking failed, refunds are automatic within 5-7 business days.

## Exceptions

Refund timelines may be extended during natural disasters, government travel restrictions, or airline/hotel insolvency. In such cases, Tripsaarthi will communicate revised timelines via email.
