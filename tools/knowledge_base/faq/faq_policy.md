# Tripsaarthi Frequently Asked Questions

## Booking

**Q: How far in advance can I book a flight?**
A: Flights can be booked up to 11 months in advance, depending on airline schedule releases.

**Q: Can I modify my travel dates after booking?**
A: Yes, date changes are allowed subject to fare difference and an airline change fee, which varies by airline and fare type.

**Q: Do you support multi-city itineraries?**
A: Yes, Tripsaarthi supports multi-city flight bookings with up to 5 stops in a single itinerary.

## Payments

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: We accept credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and popular wallets. EMI options are available on bookings above ₹3,000.

**Q: Is it safe to save my card details?**
A: Yes, all payment data is tokenized and stored per PCI-DSS compliance standards. Tripsaarthi does not store raw card numbers.

**Q: Why was I charged but didn't get a booking confirmation?**
A: This usually happens due to a temporary gateway delay. If a booking fails after payment, the amount is automatically refunded within 5-7 business days.

## Account & Support

**Q: How do I reach customer support?**
A: In-app chat is available 24/7. Phone support is available from 8 AM to 10 PM IST.

**Q: Can I book for someone else using my account?**
A: Yes, you can add traveler details different from your account profile at the time of booking.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Note that active bookings must be completed or cancelled before account deletion.

## Travel Documents

**Q: Do I need a visa for international bookings made through Tripsaarthi?**
A: Visa requirements depend on destination and traveler nationality. Tripsaarthi shows visa guidance during checkout but does not process visas directly.

**Q: Is travel insurance included?**
A: Travel insurance is optional and can be added during checkout for an additional fee.
